// CONCEPT & SYSTEMS DESIGN

Red Project: Economic Simulation Prototype

An exploration of emergent systems, resource management, and economy balancing. Conceived as a macro-management experience set in ancient Athens, this project strips away traditional narrative to focus purely on complex, interconnected mechanics. The core challenge was to design a banking legislature system where the environment reacts dynamically to the player's rules. Currently archived as a comprehensive 14-page Game Design Document (GDD) while I prioritize active development cycles. Though not in active production, it remains a foundational study in my approach to mathematical balancing and systems architecture for Unity 2D.
